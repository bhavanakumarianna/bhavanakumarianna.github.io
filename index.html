About

Engineer who owns the system, from design to on-call.

I'm a Software Engineer with 2+ years of experience building cloud-based applications, distributed systems and scalable platform services. I mostly write Java and Python backends, ship them as microservices on Kubernetes, and run them on Azure.

Lately I've been building AI into the platform itself: LLM agents that diagnose incidents, and embedding-based search that makes millions of log lines easy to search. I also serve as on-call DRI, so I care about the systems I build actually holding up in production.

Before Cisco, I built enterprise backend services at Infosys and taught Data Structures, Operating Systems and Distributed Systems as a Graduate Teaching Assistant at Kennesaw State, where I earned my M.S. in Computer Science.

What I'm looking for
